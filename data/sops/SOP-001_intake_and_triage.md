# SOP-001 Safety Intake and Triage (Fictional Training SOP)

This fictional standard operating procedure describes how intake operators record incoming product quality complaints and suspected adverse event reports received by email or telephone. It is training material only and does not describe any real company process.

## Scope

Applies to all intake operators handling fictional reports in the Safety Intake and Triage Tracker. The tracker records information only. Intake operators never assess causality, never recommend medical treatment and never decide regulatory reportability; those activities belong to qualified pharmacovigilance staff in validated enterprise systems.

## Mandatory Fields

Every report must capture the report type, product name, batch or lot number in the format LOT-##### (for example LOT-88392), the date the report was received, the reporter type and a description. Suspected adverse event reports must additionally capture an event description and an event onset date. The event onset date cannot be later than the date received. Reports missing any mandatory field cannot be saved.

## Data Minimisation

Do not record patient names, initials, dates of birth, addresses, telephone numbers, email addresses or medical record numbers. Record the reporter only as a reporter type: Healthcare Professional, Patient/Consumer, Sales Representative or Other. If a caller volunteers personal details, do not transcribe them.

## Reference Numbers

The tracker automatically assigns a unique reference number in the format REF-YYYY-NNNN when a report is saved. Quote this reference in all follow-up communication. Reference numbers are never reused.

## Triage Timeliness

New reports should be moved to In Review within one business day of receipt so that a reviewer can check completeness. Telephone intake should be entered on the same day the call is received.
