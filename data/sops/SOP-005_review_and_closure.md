# SOP-005 Review Workflow and Closure Approval (Fictional Training SOP)

Fictional procedure for moving reports through the tracker lifecycle.

## Status Lifecycle

Every report moves through three statuses: New, In Review and Closed. The lifecycle is one-way: a New report can be moved to In Review, and an In Review report can only move on to Closed through the closure approval below. Reports are never moved backwards; if information is missing, the reviewer records a follow-up note and keeps the report In Review. Closed is a final status and cannot be reopened in the tracker.

## Closure Approval (Human in the Loop)

A report can only be closed after a closure request is submitted and a Safety Reviewer approves it. While a closure request is pending the report is frozen and no other status change is possible. The reviewer approving closure must be a different person from the operator who requested it (four-eyes principle). The reviewer may reject the request, which returns the report to In Review.

## What Closure Means

Closing a report in the tracker only means the intake record is complete and has been handed over. Closure is not a clinical or regulatory decision and does not mean that an event has been assessed.

## Reopening

Because Closed is final, any new information about a closed report is logged as a new report that quotes the original reference number in its description.
