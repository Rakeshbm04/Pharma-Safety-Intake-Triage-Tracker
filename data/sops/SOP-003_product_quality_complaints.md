# SOP-003 Product Quality Complaint Handling (Fictional Training SOP)

Fictional procedure for product quality complaints (PQC) such as defects in tablets, vials, packaging or labelling.

## Typical Complaint Categories

Product quality complaints include tablet discoloration or colour change, broken or chipped tablets, crumbling tablets, visible particles or particulate matter in a solution, cracked vials, leaking containers, damaged packaging, a broken seal, a torn blister, an unusual odor, and labelling errors such as a missing or incorrect label.

## Required Details

Record the product name, the exact batch or lot number printed on the pack (format LOT-#####), the date received and a clear description of the defect. Event description and event onset date are not used for product quality complaints. If a complaint also mentions a suspected adverse event, log a separate Suspected Adverse Event report.

## Sample Retention

Ask the reporter to retain the affected pack and any remaining product so the quality team can request it for investigation. Do not ask the reporter to send samples until the quality team issues a return kit.

## Escalation to Quality

Complaints involving particulate matter in an injectable product, a cracked vial or a suspected counterfeit pack are moved to In Review on the day of receipt so that the quality team can start a batch investigation.
