# SOP-004 Batch and Lot Trend Review (Fictional Training SOP)

Fictional procedure describing how reviewers look for repeated reports against the same batch.

## Batch Lookups

Reviewers search the tracker by batch or lot number (for example LOT-88392 or LOT-45127) to list all historical reports recorded against that batch. The search returns both product quality complaints and suspected adverse event reports so that the quality team sees the complete intake history for a lot.

## Trend Signal for Quality Review

When three or more product quality complaints describing the same defect are recorded against one batch within thirty days, the reviewer notifies the quality team so that a root cause investigation can be opened. Counting complaints is an administrative trend check; it is not a safety signal assessment.

## Root Cause Investigation

The quality team, not the intake operator, performs the root cause investigation, reviews manufacturing records for the batch and decides on any corrective and preventive action. The tracker only records that a referral to quality was made.
